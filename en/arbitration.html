The Local Bridge

Navigating Turkish Law in International Proceedings

In complex international arbitrations, the interaction between the applicable law and the procedural requirements of the seat—especially when the dispute involves Turkish assets, entities, or jurisdiction—requires a precise understanding of both international standards and local mandates.

Özgören Law Firm acts as the essential local partner for international firms, providing the necessary legal bridge to ensure that the proceedings are not only compliant with institutional rules (such as ICC, HKIAC, or UNCITRAL) but also resilient against future challenges in Turkish courts.

Technical Insight: Mandatory Law & Public Policy

One of the most critical aspects of co-counsel support in Turkey is the analysis of mandatory rules (jus cogens) and the public policy (ordre public) exception. Even if a tribunal applies a foreign law, the final award must not violate the public policy of Turkey to be enforceable. Our role is to ensure that the strategy adopted during the proceedings safeguards the award's future enforceability under the New York Convention and Turkish law.
