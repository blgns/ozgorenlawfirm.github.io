Selin Özgören — İzmir, Turkey

Özgören Law Firm accepts instructions in international commercial arbitration and investment arbitration proceedings. Based in Izmir, acting globally in ICC, HKIAC, UNCITRAL, LCIA, and ISTAC proceedings.

Assisting clients in the resolution of complex disputes and in the prevention of future conflicts through precision and careful structuring. Fluent in English and Turkish with experience across Europe, Asia, Africa, and North America.

Profile

Legal Excellence & Global Connectivity

Selin Özgören focuses on international commercial arbitration and investment arbitration, as well as cross-border disputes involving international trade law and public international law.

She holds an LL.M. from Bocconi University, examining sustainable development and international investment arbitration. She obtained her LL.B. from Ankara University Faculty of Law.

Professional experience includes the Secretariat of UNCITRAL in Vienna (Working Group III on ISDS reform), case administration at the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre (HKIAC), and arbitration cases at a Milan-based law firm. She has worked on cross-border regulatory matters at Allen & Overy and Baker McKenzie.
