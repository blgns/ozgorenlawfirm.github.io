About

A Global Legal Practice
Rooted in Integrity

Selin Özgören is the founder of Özgören Law Firm and focuses on international commercial and investment arbitration, as well as cross-border disputes involving international trade law, and public international law. She is admitted to the Izmir Bar Association and has experience across both common law and civil law jurisdictions.

She holds an LL.M. from Bocconi University, completed on a full scholarship, where she wrote her thesis on the intersection of sustainable development and international investment arbitration. She obtained her law degree from Ankara University Faculty of Law, including an Erasmus Exchange year at University of Bologna.

Her professional experience includes internship work with the Secretariat of UNCITRAL in Vienna, contributing to Working Group III on ISDS reform, and case administration support at the Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre. She has also been involved in arbitration cases at a Milan-based law firm and has gained experience in cross-border regulatory and sanctions-related matters at international law firms, including Allen & Overy and Baker McKenzie. In the United States, she has worked on legal proceedings before federal authorities in New York and California.

She is bilingual in English and Turkish and is a sworn Turkish–English translator certified by the Ministry of Justice. Selin Özgören remains actively engaged in the international arbitration community as a former participant, coach, and arbitrator in the Willem C. Vis International Commercial Arbitration Moot, and currently serves as Co-Chair of the International Liaisons Team of the Vienna based Moot Alumni Association.
